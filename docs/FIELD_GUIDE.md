# Field guide

1. Confirm that the restroom exists and record its exact building and level as posted. Do not infer floor numbers from map labels.
2. Record a safe landmark, entrance approach, access encountered, and whether a Columbia ID was required at that visit. Do not publish access codes or bypass routes.
3. Check accessibility at the restroom itself. A building entrance does not establish restroom accessibility.
4. Use `/admin/inspect` for a repeat visit to an existing bathroom. Do not create a second location just to add another report.
5. Rate cleanliness, privacy, and upkeep from 1 to 5. Mark paper, soap, queue, and closures unknown if you did not see them.
6. Do not photograph people or restroom interiors. Avoid identifiable visitor details in notes.
7. Owner: review potential duplicates in `/admin/bathrooms`, then publish only sourced records with clear uncertainty labels.

Before a private beta, verify at least 10 distinct restrooms in 5 buildings. Recheck the ten most-used locations weekly when practical. For public launch, target 20–30 locations in at least 10 buildings and have testers try at least ten routes end to end.
