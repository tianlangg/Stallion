## Building access

[Columbia Housing's residence hall access policy](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/content/residence-hall-access) says authorized undergraduate Housing residents use their CUID for undergraduate residence halls, except brownstones; brownstones are limited to their residents and designated communities. [538 W. 114th Street](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/content/538-w-114th-street) is a brownstone. The policy does not publish daily opening or closing times for these halls. Columbia's [2024 security report](https://publicsafety.columbia.edu/sites/default/files/content/SecurityReport2024.pdf) describes 24-hour access control for undergraduate halls; that does not mean public entry is available around the clock. App labels reflect the access policy, not public opening hours.

## Main-page floor overview (October 22, 2026)

Floor labels come from the floor-plan links on each [Columbia Housing hall page](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/housing-options/residence-hall-directory). The app shows every linked residential floor at once. Exact counts and gender breakdowns were read from the linked plan for each floor where labels were clear. A `+` count is a **minimum**, inferred only from Housing's statement that bathrooms are on each floor (`multiple` means at least two). None of these is a live availability count.

- [Schapiro](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/schapiro): floor plans 2–16 show four bathrooms on floors 2–9 (one men's, two women's, one single-use ADA) and two on floors 10–16 (one men's, one women's). The floor 17 plan has no bathroom and is omitted.
- [Broadway](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/broadway): floor plans 3–13 show six bathrooms each: one men's, one women's, and four gender-neutral single-use ADA rooms. Floor 6 is all-female; its two shared bathrooms are women's, plus four gender-neutral rooms.
- [Furnald](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/furnald): floor plans 2–10 show one men's and one women's bathroom each.
- [Wien](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/wien): floor plans show two bathrooms on floor 2 with no gender labels; floors 3–9 and 12 each have one men's, one women's, and one ADA single-use room; floors 10–11 have one men's and one women's room.
- [River](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/river): four single-use bathrooms are visible on each floor plan 1–6; gender designation is not shown.
- [John Jay](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/johnjay), [Wallach](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/wallach), [538 W. 114th](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/content/538-w-114th-street), and [619 W. 113th](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/content/619-w-113th-street): Housing says bathrooms are on each linked floor but the app has no reliable per-floor shared count; shown as 1+.
- [McBain](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/mcbain): Housing says multiple bathrooms on each linked floor; shown as 2+.
- [611 W. 112th](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/611): Housing explicitly gives seven single-use bathrooms on each of floors 1–6; shown as an exact published count.
- [Harmony](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/harmony) and [600 W. 113th](https://www.housing.columbia.edu/600west113): Housing lists floor plans but does not clearly identify a public/shared bathroom count on every floor. Each floor shows `?` rather than an invented number or availability claim.

Private suite bathrooms are excluded from the floor counts. Where a floor plan does not label gender, the app leaves designation unknown instead of treating single-use as all-gender. Floor-plan counts and labels still need an in-person check for current access and condition.
